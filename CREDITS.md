# Credits

NorthernCrater is a mod manager and launcher. The game engine it builds and runs is the work of others:

- **GJ Materia** by Good JOE (<https://github.com/goodjoeth/gj-materia>, GPL-3.0): the macOS port of FF7Recomp. The `engine/` folder is a copy of its engine sources (commit `2b58834`), under the same license, with the changes listed in `engine/NOTICE.md`. It is built on the user's Mac.
- **FF7Recomp** by bhuiyah (<https://github.com/bhuiyah/ff7-recomp-android>, GPL-3.0): FINAL FANTASY VII statically recompiled, which GJ Materia is based on.
- **FFNx** by Julian Xhokaxhiu and contributors (GPL-3.0): the renderer and game patches.
- **pcrecomp** by sp00nznet, **bgfx / bx / bimg** by Branimir Karadzic and the FFNx forks, **SDL3**, **FFmpeg**.
- **7th Heaven** and its catalogs by the Tsunamods team (<https://github.com/tsunamods-codes>): the `.iro` mod format and mod catalogs.
